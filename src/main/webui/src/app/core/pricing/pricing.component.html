Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my progress if I do not pay?

Your progress is stored locally in your browser. If you are on the Free plan, you can continue learning indefinitely. When you are ready to certify, you can upgrade and complete your certificate at any time.

Can I switch from Per Certificate to Unlimited?

Yes. If you have purchased individual certificates, contact us and we can apply those credits toward an annual subscription.

Is my certificate really verified?

Yes. Each certificate has a unique verification link that employers can click to confirm your achievement and see exactly what skills you demonstrated.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and process payments securely through Stripe.
